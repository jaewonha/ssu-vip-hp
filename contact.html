For Prospective Students

SSU VIP Lab. is looking for strongly self-motivated graduate students to develop their careers. We will schedule a short interview before applying. It is recommended that you first apply for an internship or undergraduate research program before applying to the graduate programs.

Undergraduate Research Program

  1. Paper selection — Given a set of top-tier conference papers, you first choose one topic of interest to you.
  2. Preparation — For each semester & vacation, you will understand the selected papers (i.e., motivation, method, and contributions).
  3. Presentation — You will present it in front of VIP members and discuss research ideas, implementation issues, future directions, etc.

We are also open to conducting university-industry collaborations. If you are interested in our lab, please feel free to contact us.
