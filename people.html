I am an associate professor at the School of AI Convergence, Soongsil University (2020~Present). I received M.S and Ph.D degrees in electrical engineering from KAIST, South Korea in 2010 and 2018, respectively. I received a B.S degree in electrical and electronic engineering from Yonsei University, South Korea in 2008. During my graduate study, I worked at the Robotics and Computer Vision (RCV) Lab with Prof. In So Kweon and Yu-Wing Tai. For industrial applications, I had worked at the System IC (SIC) Center, LG Electronics (2011~2014), and Intelligent Information R&D Division, Korea Electronics Technology Institute (KETI) (2017~2020).
