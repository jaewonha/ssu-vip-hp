Welcome to VIP Lab. at Soongsil University, South Korea. We are working on visual intelligence and its applications to automated systems. We cover a variety of issues in the field of image processing, computer vision & graphics, robotics, and machine learning. Our research can be applied to practical software and lab-to-real platforms such as autonomous vehicles, smart home & factory, medical applications, intelligent semiconductors, and any other AI systems.
